## Me in a Nutshell
I'm a people-first engineer with 10+ years experience in consumer-facing web application development. I don't love technology for its own sake, but rather for what it can enable users to do. (Which inevitably means I am equally worried about what it enables powerful interests to do.) My approach means I'm flexible on implementation but stubborn on product vision. Even more than shipping product, I'm proudest of what my teams build through trust and kaizen. The most rewarding part of my role is coaching and developing others and seeing them succeed.
