## Me in a Nutshell
I'm a people-first engineer with 10+ years experience in consumer-facing web application development. I don't love technology for its own sake, but rather for what it can enable users to do. (Which inevitable means I am equally worried about what tech enables powerful interests to do.) My product-leaning approach means I'm flexible on implementation but stubborn on vision. Even more than shipping product I'm proud of the team we have created through years of kaizen. The most rewarding part of my role is coaching and developing others and seeing them succeed.
