## [Amazon Ipsum](https://amazon-ipsum.29poms.com/)

A lorem ipsum generator using Amazon speak.

Powered by [meta-ipsum](https://github.com/vegetabill/meta-ipsum), a simple framework that let's anyone define a lorem ipsum by providing a vocabulary. I was surprised one didn't already exist.

Live at [https://amazon-ipsum.29poms.com/](https://amazon-ipsum.29poms.com/)

### Tech

- [meta-ipsum library](https://github.com/vegetabill/meta-ipsum)
- [React demo site](https://github.com/vegetabill/amazon-ipsum)
